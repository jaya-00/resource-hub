Mental health resources

You are not alone. If you or a friend need support, talk to a school counselor or a trusted adult. These lines are free and open any time.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call or text 988.
  • Crisis Text Line. Text HOME to 741741.
  • The Trevor Project (for LGBTQ+ young people). Call 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678.
  • In an emergency, call 911.
